Bedrock for Mac 2.0.5 supports the newest Claude, GPT, and Grok models on Amazon Bedrock and fixes requests that Bedrock rejected for them.

- **Claude Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, and Haiku 5.5:** Recognize each model with its own thinking controls, a 128,000-token output limit, tools, documents, images, and prompt caching. Turning **Thinking** off no longer fails: Sonnet 5.5 uses Bedrock's between-tools mode and Haiku 5.5 disables thinking, both at High effort or lower. Opus 5.5 always thinks, so it keeps your selected effort. Haiku 5.5 previously had no tools, documents, prompt caching, or reasoning controls, and a 4,096-token output limit.
- **GPT-6.2 Astra, GPT-6.2 Sol, GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6.1 Terra, GPT-6 Sol, and GPT-6 Luna:** Turning Thinking off no longer sends an Off effort to GPT-6.2 and GPT-6.1 Sol, which reject it; their reasoning stays on at your selected effort. GPT-6.1 Terra, GPT-6 Sol, and GPT-6 Luna can still turn reasoning off. Choose effort up to Very high or Max, use up to 128,000 output tokens, and attach documents through Bedrock Runtime Responses. GPT-6.2 cannot read PDF files on Bedrock: the app explains this before sending, and earlier PDFs in a conversation are noted instead of sent.
- **Grok 4.7 and 4.6:** Choose reasoning effort from Low to Very high, use up to 128,000 output tokens, and attach documents to Grok 4.7.
- **GPT-5.4 and GPT-5.5:** Use their Bedrock Runtime inference profiles in Regions where bedrock-mantle does not serve them, such as GPT-5.5 in US West (Oregon). Regions served by bedrock-mantle keep their existing route.
- **Data retention:** When Bedrock refuses a model because of your account's data retention mode in the current Region, explain the setting and link to the AWS guide. OpenAI GPT models on Bedrock Runtime and Claude Fable models require a mode other than `none`.
- **Model catalog:** Update the bundled catalog to the models available on November 23, 2026, including Kimi K3.1, GLM 5.3, Pegasus 1.5, and Nova 2.5 Sonic, and remove models that AWS moved to Legacy. Live discovery still determines what your account and Region can use.

Validation covers model classification, serialized Converse and Responses requests, reasoning on and off, saved settings, output limits, document routing, PDF replay, and error messages. Live Bedrock checks verified each new model's accepted reasoning settings, output limits, tool calls, and text and PDF inputs in US East (N. Virginia) and US West (Oregon). Kimi K3.1 and GLM 5.3 use the generic request path; their live behavior was not verified because the test account has no quota for them.

Update using **Check for Updates**, or download **Amazon.Bedrock.Client.for.Mac.dmg** below. If you are running 2.0.0 or 2.0.1, close the app and install from the DMG; those versions have a shutdown bug that can prevent automatic installation from restarting the app.

Supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs on macOS 14 or later.
